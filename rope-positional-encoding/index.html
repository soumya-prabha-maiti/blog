RoPE - Rotary Position Embedding

Unlike sinusoidal PE (added at input), RoPE is applied inside every attention layer by rotating query and key vectors.

For each pair of dimensions \((2i, 2i+1)\), the query/key vector is rotated by angle \(\theta_i \cdot pos\):

\[\begin{pmatrix} q'_{2i} \\ q'_{2i+1} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos(pos \cdot \theta_i) & -\sin(pos \cdot \theta_i) \\ \sin(pos \cdot \theta_i) & \cos(pos \cdot \theta_i) \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} q_{2i} \\ q_{2i+1} \end{pmatrix}\]
where \(\theta_i = \frac{1}{10000^{2i/d}}\). The dot product \(q' \cdot k'\) then depends only on relative position \((pos_q - pos_k)\).
