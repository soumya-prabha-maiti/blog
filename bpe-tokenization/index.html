BPE Tokenization Visualizer

Byte Pair Encoding learns a subword vocabulary from a corpus. Every word starts as a sequence of characters (plus an end-of-word marker, shown as ·). At each training step, the algorithm finds the most frequent adjacent symbol pair in the corpus and merges it into a single new token, adding a merge rule to an ordered list. To tokenize new text, the learned rules are replayed in the same order. Frequent words end up as single tokens; rare words break into subword pieces — no word is ever "unknown".
